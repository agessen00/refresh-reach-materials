Let's talk about your home.

One question — we'll show you where to start.

How would you best describe the condition of your home and its effect on your health?

A

My home or surroundings are unsafe or unhealthy: mold, pests, toxins, unsafe temperatures, structural issues, poor air.

B

My home has ongoing problems that cause discomfort, stress, or health issues.

C

My home is mostly healthy, but I rely on support to keep it that way.

D

My home is consistently safe, clean, and healthy, with routines that prevent problems.

E

My home is stable and healthy, and I maintain it for the long term.

How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 2 to 7 days. Coach tag: #hazard-out. Top resources: CHAP, ODH, code enforcement, county health department, findhelp.org/housing, Red Cross shelter finder, LawHelp for tenant rights.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #healthier-home. Top resources: CHAP, findhelp.org/housing, LawHelp, healthy home education, EPA healthy homes resources.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #my-home-plan. Top resources: Healthy home education, utility management education, health department Healthy Homes programs, KOFEtime housing help.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #home-ready. Top resources: Whirlpool partner discount and appliance savings, Red Cross shelter finder, Cooperative Extension, public health alerts.
