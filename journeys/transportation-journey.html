How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 3 days to 2 weeks. Coach tag: #ride-now. Top resources: findhelp.org/transit, ODH transportation resources, transit vouchers, Medicaid transport, 211.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: 2 weeks to 30 days. Coach tag: #ride-steady. Top resources: CCMEP, Job Readiness, findhelp.org/transit for discounted fares, carpool networks.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #my-wheels. Top resources: Transportation planning and support, KOFE coaching and the credit score calculator for financing, Enrich for the budget.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #keep-moving. Top resources: LifeMart discounts on insurance, maintenance and AAA; Enrich; volunteer ride programs.
