How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: days to 30 days. Coach tag: #food-need. Top resources: Feeding America locator, 211.org, SNAP application, Meals on Wheels, local church pantries via findhelp.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: weeks to a month. Coach tag: #food-stretch. Top resources: SNAP-Ed, Cooking Matters, Double Up Food Bucks, Good and Cheap cookbook, local food co-ops.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: 2 to 3 months. Coach tag: #food-plan. Top resources: CCMEP, CareerOneStop, Goodwill Career Services, MyPlate, Healthify, MyNextMove.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #give-back. Top resources: RD locator (eatright.org), USDA farmers markets, CSA networks, Feeding America volunteer portal.
