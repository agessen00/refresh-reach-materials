How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 1 to 2 weeks, weekly. Coach tag: #first-step. Top resources: Goodwill career center locator and career resources, GoodWorks, ODH, Job Readiness, OOD, WIOA, CCMEP, MyNextMove, findhelp.org/work.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: 2 weeks to 30 days. Coach tag: #steady-work. Top resources: Skills to Succeed Academy, OOD, WIOA, SCSEP, CCMEP, findhelp.org/transit and /care, LawHelp, Goodwill career resources.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #more-income. Top resources: Job Readiness, MyNextMove, Coursera, edX, Udemy, Skills to Succeed Academy, KOFE coaching, Enrich.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 60 days to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #lead-on. Top resources: Microsoft Office discount, Northstar, ed2go, retirement planning tools, Medical Mutual, LifeMart, mentoring at Goodwill career centers.
