One question — we'll show you where to start.

How well are you able to communicate with others and get the tools you need (phone, internet, computer, language support)?

A

I can't communicate well. I don't have a working phone, internet, or computer, or I can't understand the information I need.

B

I try to communicate but often struggle with devices, internet, or language.

C

I can communicate but need help with technology or understanding information.

D

I communicate well and have the tools I need most of the time.

E

I have strong communication skills and full access to the tools and support I need.

How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 1 to 7 days. Coach tag: #connect-first. Top resources: findhelp.org/money for phone and internet bill help, findhelp.org/goods for devices, public library computers, OOD support services.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #tools-steady. Top resources: CCMEP, WIOA, Northstar Digital Literacy, ESL classes, Duolingo, Mango.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #digital-skills. Top resources: Northstar Digital Literacy, Khan Academy, LearnFree, Mango, Duolingo.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #stay-connected. Top resources: Microsoft Office discount, Skills to Succeed Academy, public library programs, Mango.
