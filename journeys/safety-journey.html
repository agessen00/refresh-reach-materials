Let's talk about feeling safe.

One question — we'll show you where to start.

How would you best describe your current level of safety for yourself and your family, at home, in your surroundings, and in the community?

A

I feel unsafe because of threats, violence, or dangerous conditions where I live or spend time.

B

I have ongoing safety concerns or unpredictable environments.

C

I feel mostly safe but rely on supports to reduce risks or manage safety issues.

D

I feel consistently safe, with routines and practices that keep me and my home safe.

E

I feel very safe and keep strong habits that prevent risk for me and my family long term.

How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 1 to 7 days. Coach tag: #safe-now. Top resources: Domestic Violence Hotline, RAINN, Red Cross shelter finder, victim advocacy, LawHelp, findhelp.org/housing.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #safer-days. Top resources: CHAP, ODH, community policing and neighborhood programs, violence prevention workshops, LawHelp.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #my-safety. Top resources: Safety planning resources, community violence prevention programs, self-defense classes, Psychology Today therapist finder.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #stay-safe. Top resources: Community support networks, neighborhood associations, Red Cross shelter finder, emergency preparedness.
