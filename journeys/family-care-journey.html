How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 2 to 7 days. Coach tag: #home-safe. Top resources: ODH, findhelp.org/goods and /care, the National Parent & Youth Helpline, caregiver support.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #home-routine. Top resources: CCMEP, GoodWorks, CHAP, Institute on Aging, aging-care support, the National Parent & Youth Helpline.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #home-steady. Top resources: Family caregiver education, aging-care support, findhelp.org/care, Institute on Aging.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #home-strong. Top resources: Institute on Aging supports, CHAP, LawHelp for family legal papers, We Conceive for family planning.
