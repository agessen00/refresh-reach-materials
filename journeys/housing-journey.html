A

How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 48 hours to 2 weeks. Coach tag: #housing-crisis. Top resources: 211.org, HUD Coordinated Entry, local shelters via findhelp, emergency rental assistance.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + prevention. Timeframes: weeks to a month. Coach tag: #housing-risk. Top resources: emergency rental assistance, HUD Housing Counseling, Legal Aid eviction defense, tenant-rights classes.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: 1 to 6 months. Coach tag: #housing-plan. Top resources: WIOA, Goodwill Career Services, rent-reporting / credit building, affordable housing listings.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 months to annual, ongoing. Coach tag: #give-back. Top resources: HUD homebuyer education, NeighborWorks, down-payment assistance, Habitat for Humanity.
