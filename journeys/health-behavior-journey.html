How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 3 days to 2 weeks. Coach tag: #basics-first. Top resources: I Am Sober, the A.A. Meeting Guide, SAMHSA helpline, findhelp.org/food and /health, MyPlate.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #small-wins. Top resources: MyPlate, MyFitnessPal, FitOn, yoga and workout videos, I Am Sober, A.A. Meeting Guide, smoking cessation programs, NeedyMeds for cessation medicines.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #stay-steady. Top resources: Lose It!, Healthify, Nike Training Club, FitOn, I Am Sober, Headspace, quit smoking programs.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #stay-well. Top resources: LifeMart discounts on memberships, dental and vision; Nike Training Club; MyFitnessPal; Eventbrite; community recreation.
