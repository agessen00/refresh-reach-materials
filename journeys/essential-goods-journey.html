How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 3 to 14 days. Coach tag: #basics-in-hand. Top resources: findhelp.org/goods, emergency clothing closets and clothing banks, 211, safe-sleep crib programs.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: 30 to 60 days. Coach tag: #steady-supply. Top resources: Diaper banks, hygiene pantries and clothing closets via findhelp.org/goods, WIOA, back-to-school drives, community swaps.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #ready-to-replace. Top resources: KOFEtime budgeting, Enrich, Consumer.gov worksheets, furniture banks, appliance savings.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #share-what-you-have. Top resources: Credit unions, KOFE coaching, Whirlpool and LifeMart discounts, volunteer programs, Eventbrite volunteer events.
