How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 1 to 7 days. Coach tag: #support-now. Top resources: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, the National Parent & Youth Helpline, Psychology Today therapist finder, Teladoc, Calm, Headspace.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #steady-days. Top resources: NAMI, Psychology Today therapist finder, support groups, the Understanding Anxiety video series, Headspace, Calm, yoga videos.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #my-tools. Top resources: Headspace, Calm, NAMI, counseling, peer support groups, yoga and workout videos.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #stay-strong. Top resources: Headspace and Calm continued use, FitOn, yoga videos, Eventbrite for local activities.
