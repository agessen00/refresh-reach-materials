How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 7 days to 2 weeks. Coach tag: #bills-first. Top resources: findhelp.org/money, 211, GoodWorks, ODH, SNAP state directory, KOFE coaching in English or Spanish.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #budget-steady. Top resources: KOFEtime budgeting, credit and housing help; Enrich money courses; CCMEP, Job Readiness, SCSEP, OOD; credit union second-chance accounts.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #build-savings. Top resources: KOFE coaching, Enrich, the credit score calculator, MyNextMove, Skills to Succeed Academy, nonprofit credit counseling.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #grow-wealth. Top resources: LifeMart discounts, Whirlpool partner discount, planning tools (retirement, student loans, home affordability), LawHelp for wills and estates.
