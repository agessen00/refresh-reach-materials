How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 3 days to 2 weeks. Coach tag: #one-person. Top resources: National Parent & Youth Helpline, 988, RAINN, NAMI support groups, findhelp.org/care, Eventbrite, libraries.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #show-up. Top resources: Eventbrite, OOD, NAMI, LawHelp, community and belonging groups, the Understanding Anxiety video series.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #my-people. Top resources: Peer support groups, advocacy training, Eventbrite, Psychology Today therapist finder, community engagement opportunities.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #belong. Top resources: Ongoing community support networks, Eventbrite, volunteer events, NAMI, sport ticket discounts for outings.
