Let's talk about getting care.

One question — we'll show you where to start.

How well are you able to get the health care you need, for yourself or your family?

A

I can't get health care right now, or I don't have a doctor I can go to.

B

I can get some care, but cost, rides, or other things often get in the way.

C

I get care when I need it but need help staying consistent or understanding what to do.

D

I get regular care, follow medical advice, and keep up with appointments.

E

I take charge of my health, keep regular checkups, and know how to use the health system.

How the four stages differ — tone, resources, and coach tags

Stabilize · Level 1

Tone: urgent + reassuring. Timeframes: 1 to 7 days. Coach tag: #care-now. Top resources: Teladoc, NeedyMeds clinics and the free drug discount card, ODH, CHAP, Medicaid application help, findhelp.org/health.

Connect · Level 2

Tone: stability + expansion. Timeframes: about 30 days. Coach tag: #care-routine. Top resources: CHAP, ODH, Teladoc, NeedyMeds clinic finder, the Diabetes Essentials video series, clinic health educators.

Build Skills · Level 3

Tone: growing agency. Timeframes: monthly to 90 days. Coach tag: #care-confident. Top resources: Medical, dental and vision benefits education, NeedyMeds discount card, findhelp.org/transit and /care, CDC.gov.

Maintain · Level 4 & 5

Tone: resilience + giving back. Timeframes: 3 to 6 months, ongoing. Coach tag: #care-ahead. Top resources: Medical Mutual, Lincoln Dental, vision and life insurance, Teladoc, aging-care support, NeedyMeds.
