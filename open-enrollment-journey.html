What does each team member see in the app?

STEP 1 · 4 WEEKS BEFORE

Complete your survey

The screen says"Open enrollment runs [start date] to [end date]. Sixteen short questions, about six minutes, help us plan how to meet your needs and goals. Please speak with your manager: you can do this on the clock. Had your wellness checkup this year? Turn in the form now. If not, book it as soon as you can: your premium discount starts the month after you turn in the form."
  • Sixteen questions, one for each area of life; any can be skipped
  • Reminder to turn in your checkup form, or book the visit soon, with the physician form to download; forms are welcome all year
  • Upload a photo of the signed form; HR messages you once it's reviewed

Find what you need

Have questions?

The screen says"Get help in person or online. The calendar shows the dates and sites where HR offers one-on-one support at your location, or book a virtual time with HR."
  • Ask HR through a short question form, any hour
  • Calendar page: dates and sites for one-on-one support at each location
  • Book 15 minutes with HR by video, from the calendar
  • Join an info session or the benefits fair (+150)

Enroll

The screen says"Your 2027 choices are within reach. Enrollment closes [end date]. If you make no changes, [the plan's default]. Your wellness discount: Approved." Then the Enroll button opens your benefits site.
  • The Enroll link opens the same enrollment site that shows what you have and pay now, with no new login where single sign-on is set up; you can do this on the clock
  • Have this ready: birth dates and Social Security numbers for anyone you cover, and your doctor's name
  • Need help? One tap opens the HR booking calendar, or asks HR to call you back
  • Back in the app: "My 2027 choices are made" (+500; +200 and a drawing entry with a week to spare)

What does HR set up before enrollment opens?

8 to 6 weeks before
Settle the plan details
  • Confirm 2027 plans, rates and what changes
  • Set the start and end dates, and the default if no choice is made
  • Set the wellness credit rules: who can earn it, which document counts, how much it takes off the premium
  • Checkup forms are welcome all year; the discount starts the month after HR receives the form
  • Write the drawing rules: who is entered, the prize, and when it is drawn
6 to 4 weeks before
Set up the first step
  • Re-authorize the roster sync and turn on the daily sync; tag who is eligible and who can earn the wellness credit
  • Needs assessment, checkup reminder and the physician form with its photo upload
  • Put the start and end dates on the app calendar
  • Name the wellness reviewer and the question owner, with reply times
  • Turn on the open enrollment challenge and its points; step 1 opens at 4 weeks
4 to 2 weeks before
Build the guide and sessions
  • Digital benefits guide from the Beyond Insurance template, with this year's plan pages; it opens at 2 weeks
  • Five-minute video on choosing a plan, and the "I've looked" check-in
  • One link to the benefits enrollment site: it shows each person's current benefits and cost, and it is where they enroll
  • Calendar page for the 2 weeks before enrollment: one-on-one support by location and site, virtual HR booking sessions, info sessions and the fair
  • Confirmation check-in and pulse survey
While enrollment is open
Review, answer and celebrate
  • Review wellness uploads within three working days
  • Read the daily progress report and send the nudges it suggests
  • Compare confirmations with the enrollment site's completion list each week
  • Thank each person who confirms; post a weekly shout-out for locations whose whole team is done

What does the needs assessment ask?

Five answers each, A to E, from Know the Need. Any can be skipped. Each answer adds a tag that points step 2 to the guide pages that fit. Feeling Safe has its own pathway and a quick exit.

  1. Mental Health. How are you feeling most days?
  2. Transportation. How would you best describe your current transportation situation?
  3. Workforce Development. How would you best describe your current employment situation?
  4. Communication. Do you have a working phone and internet, and can you understand the information you get?
  5. Education Stability. Are you or your family able to meet your school or learning needs, if you have any?
  6. Environmental Health. Which statement best describes the health and safety of where you live?
  7. Family Care. How well are you able to care for the people who depend on you?
  8. Financial Stability. Which statement best describes how you manage your money and pay your bills?
  9. Health Behavior Promotion. In the past 30 days, how well have you been able to care for your health through eating, sleeping, being active, and other daily habits?
  10. Health Care Access & Quality. In the past 12 months, how well have you been able to get the health care you or your family needed?
  11. Housing. How would you best describe your current housing situation today?
  12. Nutrition. In the past 3 months, how well have you been able to get enough food for yourself and your family?
  13. Feeling Safe. In the past 30 days, have you or your family faced threats, violence, or unsafe situations at home or in places you spend time?
  14. Social Connection. In the past 30 days, how connected have you been to people you can talk to or count on for help?
  15. Legal & Civic Stability. How would you best describe any legal issues or missing documents that affect your daily life (such as ID, court matters, fines, a record, or child support)?
  16. Essential Goods. Can you get the everyday items you and your family need: clothing, shoes, hygiene products, bedding, household basics?
