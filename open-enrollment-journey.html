What does each team member see in the app?

STEP 1 · 4 WEEKS BEFORE

Complete your survey

The screen says"Open enrollment runs [start date] to [end date].
Sixteen short questions, about six minutes.
You can do this on the clock; please speak with your manager.
Had your checkup this year? Turn in the form now. If not, book it soon: your discount starts the month after."
  • Sixteen questions, one for each area of life; any can be skipped, and answers save as you go
  • Reminder to turn in your checkup form, or book the visit soon, with the physician form to download; forms are welcome all year
  • Upload a photo of the signed form; HR messages you once it's reviewed

Federal wellness rules: a "sent back" or "not met" message names another way to earn the discount, gives an HR contact, and says a personal physician's recommendation will be accommodated (DOL).
STEP 2 · 2 WEEKS BEFORE

Find what you need

The screen says"Open enrollment is coming, with some exciting changes." A 60-second video or a one-page flyer, then the guide, starting with the pages that fit your answers. Guide pages, a short video and topic sessions, matched to each person's need tags.
  • The new benefits guide: what's new for 2027, starting with the pages that fit you (+150)
  • What you can choose: the plans open to your role, and your discount status
  • What you have and pay now: the same enrollment site link you'll use to enroll shows your current benefits and cost per paycheck, then "I've looked" (+100)
  • Five-minute video on choosing a plan (+100)
STEP 3 · FROM 2 WEEKS BEFORE

Have questions?

The screen says"Get help in person or online.
See when HR is at your location, or book a video call with HR."
  • Ask HR through a short question form, any hour
  • Calendar page: dates and sites for one-on-one support at each location
  • Book 15 minutes with HR by video, from the calendar
  • Join an info session or the benefits fair (+150)
  • Start and end dates on the app calendar
STEP 4 · ENROLLMENT OPEN

Enroll

The screen says"Your 2027 choices are within reach.
Enrollment closes [end date].
If you make no changes: [the plan's default].
Your wellness discount: Approved." Then the Enroll button opens your benefits site.
  • The Enroll link opens the same enrollment site that shows what you have and pay now, with no new login where single sign-on is set up; you can do this on the clock
  • Choices save as pending on the enrollment site until you submit, so you can stop and come back
  • Have this ready: birth dates and Social Security numbers for anyone you cover, and your doctor's name
  • Need help? One tap opens the HR booking calendar, or asks HR to call you back
  • Back in the app: "My 2027 choices are made" (+500; +200 and a drawing entry with a week to spare)
  • Then the thank-you, your points, and a short survey (+150)

What does HR set up before enrollment opens?

8 to 6 weeks before
Settle the plan details
  • Confirm 2027 plans, rates and what changes
  • Set the start and end dates, and the default if no choice is made
  • Set the wellness credit rules: who can earn it, which document counts, how much it takes off the premium
  • Checkup forms are welcome all year; the discount starts the month after HR receives the form
  • Write the drawing rules: who is entered, the prize, and when it is drawn
6 to 4 weeks before
Set up the first step
  • Re-authorize the roster sync and turn on the daily sync; tag who is eligible and who can earn the wellness credit
  • Needs assessment, checkup reminder and the physician form with its photo upload
  • Put the start and end dates on the app calendar
  • Name the wellness reviewer and the question owner, with reply times
  • Turn on the open enrollment challenge and its points; step 1 opens at 4 weeks
4 to 2 weeks before
Build the guide and sessions
  • Digital benefits guide from the Beyond Insurance template, with this year's plan pages; it opens at 2 weeks
  • Five-minute video on choosing a plan, and the "I've looked" check-in
  • One link to the benefits enrollment site: it shows each person's current benefits and cost, and it is where they enroll
  • Calendar page for the 2 weeks before enrollment: one-on-one support by location and site, virtual HR booking sessions, info sessions and the fair
  • Confirmation check-in and pulse survey
While enrollment is open
Review, answer and celebrate
  • Review wellness uploads within three working days
  • Read the daily progress report and send the nudges it suggests
  • Compare confirmations with the enrollment site's completion list each week
  • Thank each person who confirms; post a weekly shout-out for locations whose whole team is done

How do we help 7 in 10 people take part?

Count it for each store
  • Pick the goal and count it for each store, so we can see which stores need more help
Give paid time
  • Put the time into each person's schedule, not only in messages; for people paid by the hour, this helps the most
Make it easy to find
  • A QR code by the time clock and in the break room, and a tablet at team meetings so people can do it on the spot
Celebrate each store
  • The first store where 7 in 10 people take part wins a team prize; thank stores by name in the app's news feed
Keep it short
  • About six minutes; any question can be skipped, and answers save as people go
Use people's own language
  • The survey and messages in Spanish or French where teams need it
To decide first
What counts as taking part
  • It could be finishing the survey, reading the guide, clicking through to the benefits enrollment site, or finishing enrollment
  • We suggest counting finished surveys and clicks through to the enrollment site: the survey is the part we run, and a click shows someone went to look at their benefits
  • Refresh can't see whether a person finished enrolling once they're on the site
To decide first
Who checks in with each store
  • The store manager, HR, or a helper chosen at each store

Which questions do we need help with?

  1. Can the upload field take a PDF, can a response carry a pending, approved or sent-back status, and can HR award points by hand?
  2. Can a form submission remove a tag, and can an audience leave out people with a tag?
  3. Can a sequence reminder repeat every few days, or is weekly the right rhythm?
  4. Is there a report of points or completion by location?
  5. Can Refresh count clicks on the enrollment link for each person?
  6. How is single sign-on set up for the enrollment link, and does a step hidden by tag drop out of a sequence?
  7. Can a form submission send a push notification to the HR admin, not only an email?
  8. Can a form save answers as the person goes, so a half-finished survey reopens where they stopped?
  9. Who re-authorizes the roster sync, and does the upload step open the phone camera?
  10. Which benefits administration features are live in insurance v1, and which are planned for v2?

Answer any question you already know; each one lets more of the journey run on its own.
